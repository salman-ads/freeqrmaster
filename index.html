Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about FreeQRMaster? We have answers.

Are these QR codes free for commercial use?

Yes! All QR codes generated on FreeQRMaster are 100% free and static. They never expire and can be printed on commercial goods, flyers, business cards, or restaurant menus without any restrictions or watermarks.

Do my generated QR codes expire?

No. Because our generator encodes your data directly into static matrix codes client-side in your browser, there are no tracking servers or redirects involved. Your QR code will work permanently as long as the underlying target link or info remains valid.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely. Everything runs locally in your browser via JavaScript. We do not store, log, or track any input values, WiFi passwords, contact cards, or URLs on any remote servers.
