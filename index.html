Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free browser-based QR code generation framework.

Are these QR codes free for lifetime commercial use?

Yes! All matrix symbols built using FreeQRMaster are completely free and static. They possess no expiration dates, trial limits, or scan restrictions, making them ideal for restaurant menus, business flyers, and industrial product boxing.

Do my generated QR codes ever expire?

No. Because our application encodes text strings directly into client-side visual matrices without any external redirection databases, your code remains perpetually active as long as the target information stays operational.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely. Processing occurs strictly inside your web browser via local JavaScript routines. We never log, save, or transmit your contact cards, WiFi keys, or destination URLs to remote servers.
